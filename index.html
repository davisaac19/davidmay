03 — Selected work

Problems I've solved

Current side project

SubZen

Subscription, card and payment-reminder manager

SubZen is a full-stack web app that stops money from leaking into forgotten subscriptions. It brings recurring payments and credit cards into one dashboard with a monthly budget, spending history, price-increase detection and automatic push and email reminders.

  • One dashboard for subscriptions, credit cards and due dates, with a monthly budget and spending history.
  • Flags price increases, plans cancellations with estimated savings, and sends push and email reminders.
  • Shared subscriptions: invite by email, split costs equally, by percentage or fixed amount, with per-member reminders.
  • Row-level security in PostgreSQL, so members never see the owner's payment method.
Architecture & technical decisions
  • Vue 3 SPA (TypeScript, Vite, Pinia) deployed on Netlify; Supabase for auth, PostgreSQL and Edge Functions.
  • 19 RPCs keep writes atomic and business rules in SQL; RLS on every domain table.
  • An hourly pg_cron job invokes a Deno Edge Function that resolves per-user lead times and suppresses duplicates.
  • Push via Pushy and email via Resend; ES/EN internationalization with persisted preference.
